EasyPay Mobile Banking User Guide

Introduction

EasyPay is a mobile banking application that allows users to transfer money within Nigeria. It also provides services such as airtime and data subscriptions, electricity bill payments, and loan services.

System Requirements

- A smartphone or tablet.
- A stable internet connection.
- A valid Nigerian phone number.
- A valid identification card.

Installation

1. Download the official EasyPay application.
2. Install the application on your device.
3. Open the application.
4. Follow the registration instructions.
How to Use EasyPay

1. Open the EasyPay mobile application.
2. Register using your valid phone number.
3. Complete the required verification process.
4. Log in to your account.
5. Select the service you want to use.
6. Follow the instructions to complete your transaction.

Available Services
- Money transfers.
- Airtime purchases.
- Mobile data subscriptions.
- Electricity bill payments.
- Loan services.
  Troubleshooting Common Problems

1. Unable to Log In

- Check that your phone number is correct.
- Ensure you have a stable internet connection.
- Confirm that you are using the correct login details.

2. Transfer Failed

- Check your account balance.
- Confirm that the recipient's account details are correct.
- Check your internet connection.
- Contact customer support if the problem continues.

3. Airtime or Data Purchase Failed

- Confirm that the phone number is correct.
- Check your account balance.
- Check your transaction history.

4. Application Not Opening

- Restart your device.
- Check your internet connection.
- Update the application if an update is available.
